Secondary route:
- WhatsApp and Viber: voice and video calls are restricted by operators;
  the desktop apps are routed so that calls go through the secondary route.
- Medium: blocked nationwide.
- Roblox (blocked since February 2026) and Discord (disrupted for two days in
  January 2026) ship switched off: enable them if you need them.
- AI, developer and social services are not listed: they serve Egypt directly.
