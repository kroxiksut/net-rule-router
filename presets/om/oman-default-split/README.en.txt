Name: Oman Default Split
Country: OM
Use case: Local traffic via primary; messengers, calling apps and platforms
blocked or restricted in Oman via secondary
Author: NetRuleRouter contributors
Checked: September 2026 (secondary route list, against public measurements and reports)
Tested on: NOT VERIFIED on a live connection in Oman

Secondary route:
- Signal, Viber, LINE, Threema, Element (Matrix), Clubhouse, VK, Mail.ru,
  Truecaller and TinyURL: blocked nationwide.
- Session, Wire, imo and Tango: blocked or restricted by some operators.
- LINE, Session and Wire desktop apps ship switched off: their process names
  are too generic to route safely. Check the name on your machine and enable.
- WhatsApp is not listed: users report its calls work directly since
  December 2024.
- AI, developer and major social services are not listed: they serve Oman
  directly.

IMPORTANT:
- Please review and adapt it for your ISP and service availability before use.
